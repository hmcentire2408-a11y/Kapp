## PROVENANCE AND PRECISION — read before using any number

Three different precision classes are mixed below. Do not quote across them.

- **[LABELED]** — the number was printed inside the bar. Exact.
- **[EST]** — read off bar height against the y-axis. Approximate, ±1 or worse
  on tall bars, and *worse still* on stacked segments, where a 1-unit green cap
  sits on a 26-unit red base. Never treat an [EST] as a count.
- **[CAPTION]** — printed in the footer line "Applications with no GPA not
  shown: N Accepted, M Denied". Exact.

**Whose school is this?** The panel says "your high school" and the platform's School/District toggle was set to **School data**. The per-school admit counts below track Morganton expectations closely, and the history shows exactly three graduating classes (2024/2025/2026), which is the number Morganton has had. **Against it:** the Acceptance-by-GPA footers all read "Showing 2021-2026 applications", a window that predates Morganton. Unresolved.


**GPA bin convention :** bins are
top-exclusive. `4.75 - 5.0` excludes 5.0; a student at exactly 5.0 falls in the
`5.0 - 5.25` bin. That is why a bin above the scale maximum is populated. The top populated bin at every school below is the 5.0 bin.

## Cautions that bind any use of this table

1. **These are cohort base rates. They are not individual probabilities.**
   `doctrine/conflicts-ledger.md` on exactly this. CLAUDE.md Default 6 forbids
   quantifying admission odds. Nothing here may be converted into one.
2. **Top-bin n is 1 to 13 at nine of eleven schools.** Georgetown's whole record
   is 5 applications. UChicago's is ~10. A single admit or denial moves those
   percentages by tens of points. Treat Georgetown, UChicago, Dartmouth and
   Williams as having no usable GPA signal at all.
3. **Denominators are applications logged in the platform, not applications
   filed.** "Unknown" may include students who added a school to a list and
   never applied. This inflates 2024 denominators most.
4. The Duke panel was cropped above the y-axis maximum in the supplied
   screenshot; its top bar height is a lower bound.
