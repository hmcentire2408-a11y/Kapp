# NCSSM Residential Profile 2025 — extracted text

Extracted 2026-09-07 from `school-context/ncssm-residential-profile-2025.pdf`.

Text below is the OCR layer of the published NCSSM Class of 2025 school profile.

Unless a line says otherwise, **statistics combine NCSSM-Durham and
NCSSM-Morganton** — the profile flags this explicitly on pages 2, 3 and 5. It is
a Class of 2025 document.

## Campus size

- **NCSSM-Morganton serves 300 residential students.** Two-year program (grades
  11-12), so a graduating class is **≈150**.
- NCSSM-Durham serves 680 → class ≈340.
- Morganton opened in 2022. "The admissions criteria and process are the same for
  both campuses, as are the graduation requirements and course rigor."
- Morganton "features data science throughout its coursework"; all Morganton
  juniors must take Introduction to Data Science.

Also: a Pass/No-Pass option exists, max two core courses across the two years.
Articulation agreements with all 16 UNC System universities award credit for NCSSM
courses without an AP exam.

## Testing — Class of 2025, both campuses

- **SAT:** 380 students (78% of the class) took it at least once. Best-score
  method. **Mean composite 1413, median 1430.** Math mean 715 / median 720. EBRW
  mean 705 / median 720. (The profile notes the composite mean/median is not
  necessarily the sum of the component mean/medians.)
- **ACT:** 177 students (36.3%) took 280 exams. Composite mean 30.7, median 31.
  English 30.8/33, Reading 32.2/34, Math 30.5/31, Science 30.4/31, STEM 30.7/31.
- **AP:** 335 students (70.1%) took 1,885 exams. Score distribution:
  **5 = 43.6%, 4 = 27.6%, 3 = 17.1%, 2 = 8.2%, 1 = 3.5%.** 97.6% of students who
  sat an AP scored 3+ on at least one.


## What the school itself advertises — "NCSSM HIGHLIGHTS", verbatim

> NCSSM was ranked the No. 1 public high school in America for 2025 by Niche.com.
>
> In the past five years, NCSSM students have won top honors in the Conrad
> Challenge, placed in the top 1% in COMAP's international undergraduate math
> modeling competition, received a Distinction Award at the Singapore
> International Mathematical and Computational Challenge, and won a National Gold
> Medal in the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards. NCSSM is one of the top schools in
> the country for student semifinalists in the Regeneron Science Talent Search
> competition, averaging seven semifinalists annually for the past 10 years,
> including eight semifinalists in 2025.

Every named distinction is STEM research or math/science competition, with one
arts exception. **No civics, debate, government, law or policy honor appears.**
This is the school's own account of what its students are known for, and it is the
backdrop against which a reader places any Morganton applicant.

## Curriculum requirements (Class of 2026)

Five core academic courses each semester of junior and senior year. Minimum
semester credits: 5 Science, 3 Mathematics, 2 English, 2 Social Science, 1 Senior
English, 1 Interdisciplinary Humanities Elective.

## Demographics (Class of 2025, both campuses)

Female 49% / Male 51%. Students from 72 NC counties and 231 high schools. The
class completed 40,787 community service hours; 60 hours of community service is a
graduation requirement.
